About This Platform

Built to Serve Sierra Leone's Youth

An open, modern web platform powering Maxim Nyansa Electronics' training, shop, E-Library, and donor engagement.

As Country Director of Maxim Nyansa Foundation Sierra Leone, Michael leads the organization's mission to empower young people through technical education and enterprise. User Guide

How to Use the Platform

For Applicants

1
Create an account

Click Apply Now at the top-right and register with your email and phone.

2
Browse and select a program

Visit the Training page, review the program details, and choose the one that fits you.

3
Submit your application

Click Apply Now on the program card, fill out the form, and submit.

4
Track your status

Check My Applications in your profile to see if you're pending, approved, or enrolled.

For Donors

1
See the impact

Visit the Impact page to see live metrics — trainees, programs, and community reach.

2
Pledge a donation

On the Donors page, fill in the pledge form with your amount and message.

3
Complete payment

You'll receive a Pay Now link — pay via card, mobile money, or bank transfer.

For Shoppers

1
Browse the shop

Filter by category, condition (new/refurbished/used), or search by name.

2
Add to cart and checkout

Click Add on any product, then proceed to checkout with your delivery details.

3
Pay and receive

Pay online via Paystack or arrange payment on delivery. We'll contact you to confirm.

For Administrators

1
Log in to /admin

Use your admin credentials to access the dashboard, KPIs, and charts.

2
Manage applications

Review, approve, reject, or enroll applicants. Download certificates for enrolled trainees.

3
Update content

Add programs, products, news posts, videos, and E-Library resources — all without coding.

4
Adjust site settings

Update brand name, contact info, social links, and impact metrics from Settings.

5
Send bulk emails

Target specific programs or statuses and send personalized messages to applicants.
