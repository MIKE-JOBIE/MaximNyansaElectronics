Who We Are

Empowering Youth Through Skills, Enterprise & Community

Maxim Nyansa Electronics Enterprises SL is a social-impact initiative under Maxim Nyansa Foundation Sierra Leone, turning unemployed young people into skilled technicians, entrepreneurs and community builders.

Foundation

Vision, Mission & Values

🎯

Our Vision

To become one of the leading youth empowerment and technical vocational institutions in Sierra Leone by transforming unemployed youths into skilled technicians and entrepreneurs.

🤝

Our Mission

To provide practical ICT and electronics repair training while building sustainable social enterprises capable of supporting long-term youth empowerment and community development.

🌱

Our Values

Integrity, affordability, technical excellence, self-reliance, and sustainability. Every graduate becomes both an entrepreneur and a contributor to their community.

What We're Building

Core Objectives

1
Train up to 80 youths annually in electronics repair and entrepreneurship.
2
Establish sustainable electronics repair enterprises across communities.
3
Reduce unemployment among disadvantaged and underserved youths.
4
Promote entrepreneurship and self-reliance at the grassroots level.
5
Reduce electronic waste through refurbishment and reuse.
6
Build a self-sustaining training and enterprise ecosystem.

Our Journey

From Idea to Enterprise

Phase One — Preparation Completed

Secured standby generator, purchased training tools & equipment, renovated the training hall, recruited trainers, and selected the first cohort of trainees. Foundational support provided by Maxim Nyansa Foundation, Climbing The Right Tree, Wessel Hebing and Vivace.

Phase Two — First Vocational Program Completed

Successfully completed the first Vocational Training Program in Electronics Repairs & Maintenance with 13 graduates. Intensive two-month training, 90% practical, real-life repair examinations, entrepreneurship mentoring. Supported by Maxim Nyansa Foundation, Climbing The Right Tree and Lufthansa Help Alliance.

Phase Three — Expansion In Progress

Establishing the main Electronics Shop, dedicated Refurbishment Center, procuring inventory, and expanding operations. Seeking support to scale from 13 to 80 trained youths per year.

Phase Four — National Reach Planned

Expand to additional communities across Sierra Leone, develop online learning resources, and build a regional network of graduate-owned enterprises.
