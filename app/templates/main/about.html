About Us

The Learning Company

Maxim Nyansa Electronics Enterprises SL is a social-impact initiative combining practical vocational training, electronics repair and refurbishment, youth enterprise development, and a planned electronics shop.

Our Vision

The Learning Company exists to create practical pathways for young people who may have limited access to conventional higher education. The model emphasizes hands-on technical competence, entrepreneurship, and the ability to earn a livelihood by providing useful repair services in local communities.

Our Structure

Three mutually reinforcing functions:

  • A training center for vocational learning and administration.
  • A refurbishment and repair function for faulty electronic equipment and practical work.
  • An electronics shop selling new or slightly used equipment, generating income to support local operations and future learning.

Sustainability

Our sustainability model combines affordable repair services, sales of electronic equipment, refurbishment and reuse, reinvestment of operating revenue, alumni mentorship and continued partnerships — moving progressively from donor-supported establishment toward a stronger locally-generated operating base while preserving the social mission.

Partners

We acknowledge the contribution of Maxim Nyansa Foundation, Climbing The Right Tree, Lufthansa Help Alliance, Wessel Hebing, Vivace and other supporters.
