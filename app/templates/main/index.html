Our Mission

Equipping Youth With Skills That Matter

Maxim Nyansa Electronics SL exists to create practical pathways for young people who may have limited access to conventional higher education.

Our model emphasizes hands-on technical competence, entrepreneurship, and the ability to earn a livelihood by providing useful repair services in local communities.

Our People

The Cohort

Young people from across Freetown preparing to learn, build, and lead. Approximately 90% of their learning is practical — they work directly with broken equipment and develop diagnostic and repair skills under supervision.

They'll return to their communities as technicians, entrepreneurs, and mentors for the next generation.
